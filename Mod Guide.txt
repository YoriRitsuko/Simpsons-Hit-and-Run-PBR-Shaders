PBR Shaders

Getting it running

Put PBR Shaders.lmlh in Lucas's Hacks folder. Reload the mods list and
enable PBR Shaders under Settings. The mod uses Lucas 1.27.1 and the
English retail PC game.

Want to see what it does? Put PBR Shader Demo.lmlm in Mods and enable it
under General. Start Level 1 and look by the Simpsons house. The chrome
ball shows the reflections, the cyan light shows the color bounce, and
the framed window shows the fake room moving behind the glass.

The demo maps come in the LMLM, inside Pure3D files. If you're upgrading,
replace the hack and demo together. The old VehicleShaders folder is for
materials made before packages were supported.

Tuning the look

Right-click PBR Shaders, open Mod Settings, then restart the game after
you've made your changes. With Linear HDR on, turn off Lucas's MSAA.

* Exposure changes the whole world. Sky Brightness only boosts the sky.
  The defaults are 200% and 300%. Both run from 25% to 400%.
* Bloom Strength starts at 40%. Streak Mix starts at 30%. Pull the mix
  down if you want glowing lights with shorter or softer streaks.
* Vignette starts at 65%. It dims the corners of the world before the
  HUD draws.
* Depth of Field starts with focus at 90 world units and a sharp range
  of 5. Try 4 and 5 for a closer third-person look. Foreground blur is a
  separate switch. The blur radius is measured in pixels at 1080p and
  scales with resolution.
* Histogram Equalization starts at 35%. Its strength blends between the
  tone-mapped picture and a remap based on the frame's luminance.

SSR reflects visible objects into glossy PBR surfaces. SSGI lets nearby
surfaces bounce light off each other. Ambient Occlusion makes contacts
look grounded. Use AO View > Occlusion Only to see just that effect.
The screen-space effects depend on INTZ depth. SSR and SSGI also need
four Direct3D 9 render targets.

Packing your own maps

With the source checkout, run tools/PbrTexturePacker.ps1 in PowerShell 7.
Enter the shader chunk name, choose the maps, and save a Pure3D file.
An existing ORM goes into Packed ORM. With separate maps, use the AO,
Roughness, and Metallic slots. For a window, choose Interior and use
base alpha as the window mask and room-map alpha as the emission mask.

Give the packer a model to put the materials inside it, or pack a
standalone material file and load it before the model's shader chunks.
Ship the finished Pure3D files in your LMLM through Custom Files.
RequiredHack=VehicleShaders is the stable Lucas dependency for the hack.

See docs/Materials.md in the project for the packer command, INI examples,
and export notes. For an old loose setup, keep VehicleShaders.ini beside
its existing textures until you're ready to pack them.
